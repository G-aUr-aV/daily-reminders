# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

People who want to set an everyday reminder, hear an alarm and see what happened. Some use recurring schedules, office days or reading routines.

## Product Purpose

Daily Reminders stores reminders and history locally. An alarm can be stopped or snoozed from its notification. Routine completion is recorded separately.

## Product Principles

- The common path is title, time and Save.
- Show setup actions when they are needed for a saved reminder.
- Put advanced scheduling and sound controls inside the reminder editor.
- Preserve existing reminders and history when the app is updated.
